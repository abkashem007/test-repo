Methodology

A scorecard, not a sales pitch

Every product gets the same five-part scorecard. Here’s exactly what we check and how each part is weighted.

01
Read the label

Every ingredient and dose, compared with what published research actually used.

02
Test the checkout

Upsells, auto-ship, shipping fees — we note everything you’ll see after “Buy”.

03
Check the refund

Guarantee length, who pays return shipping, and how to actually claim it.

04
Score & disclose

One score out of 10, with the cons printed as large as the pros.

The 5-point scorecard

CriterionWeightWhat earns a high score
Ingredient transparency[25%]Full label, every dose listed, no proprietary blends.
Dosing vs research[25%]Key ingredients at or near the doses used in published studies.
Refund policy[20%]Long guarantee, opened bottles accepted, clear claim process.
Price per serving[15%]Fair cost on the single bottle, not only on the bundle.
Checkout & complaints[15%]No pre-ticked auto-ship, few upsells, low complaint history.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t accept payment for a higher score or a better position.
  • We don’t publish a review without a cons list.
  • We don’t give medical advice — we review products, not your health.
